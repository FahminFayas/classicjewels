Turkish-style designs, in 21K and 22K

Turkish-style gold is heavier in form and lighter in colour than Indian work, usually 21K, with wide bangles, coin motifs and a contrast between sand-blasted and polished surfaces. The collection suits anyone who wants a single statement piece rather than a matched set.
