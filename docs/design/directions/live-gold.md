Files: `public/_proto/live-gold/index.html`, `piece.html`, `img/` (real assets only, copied from `src/assets/`, plus `streak.svg`, a drawn mask), `fonts/` (the fontsource woff2 files). ## 1. Concept and thesis

**Touchstone.** A gold trader tests metal by rubbing it on a black stone and reading the colour of the streak against needles of known karat. This direction builds the house the same way. The whole site sits on a dark stage sampled from the velvet. The one object everyone comes for, today's rate, is set as the hero in light Cormorant numerals nearly 300 px tall. Four karat needles (24, 22, 21, 18) re-price and recolour the whole page: the numerals roll to the new figure, a streak is drawn under them in that karat's colour, and the C-ring around the piece, the header chip, the touchstone card, the estimator and the action bar all follow. No competitor in the UAE does this. Of 25 sites, four publish a rate, none shows 21K by default and none shows a trend. It is honest, because every figure comes from the feed and is dated. It is tangible, because the rate leads straight to "what is my old gold worth" and "which counter do I walk into". And it reads as a gold trader's instrument made with jeweller's restraint, not a finance dashboard: one number, no red or green, no ticker, a velvet piece beside it and the brand ring drawn around it once. Pieces sit on the dark stage, ivory is for breathing, plum is for acting, and every path ends at a named branch.

## 4. Signature moments

### 4.1 The board: four needles that re-price and recolour the page

- **Mechanics.** The karat control is a native `<fieldset>` of four radio inputs styled as assay plates (karat in Cormorant, rate in Plex tabular, a 916-style stamp, a 2 px swatch bar that grows across the selected plate). CSS reads it with `:root:has(#k24:checked)` and sets `--assay` (a registered `<color>` that transitions over 600 ms) and, on every digit column, `--d: var(--d24)`. Each column is a fixed-width window (`block-size: 1em; overflow: clip`) holding a hidden width digit and a `::before` strip `"0\A 1\A … 9"` translated by `calc(var(--d) * -1em)`; columns roll 640 ms on `--ease-out` with a 45 ms stagger. The four karats' digits are baked into `style="--d24:5;--d22:4;--d21:4;--d18:3"` at build time from `src/content/rates`. **The switch needs no JavaScript.** The header chip, action bar, touchstone card, ticket and visit band show the selected karat through `.k-only.k-NN` spans toggled by the same `:has()` rule.
- **Live refresh.** `rate.ts` already fetches the feed once idle; it now writes the new `--dNN` values on each column, so a digit rolls **only when the posted value changes**, and updates the screen-reader text and "as of" time.
- **Accessibility.** The roller is `aria-hidden`; a visually hidden live region holds one sentence per karat ("22 karat gold today: AED 468.25 per gram"), and only the checked karat's sentence is displayed, so screen readers hear the change. Arrow keys move between needles natively.
- **Support and fallback.** `:has()` Chrome 105, Safari 15.4, Firefox 121; `@property` Chrome 85, Safari 16.4, Firefox 128 (older browsers swap colour instantly). Where `:has()` is missing, a 0.2 KB branch of `karat.ts` sets `data-karat` on `<html>` and the same rules key off that. With neither, the page shows 22K and the needles still list all four rates as text.
- **Reduced motion.** Digits and colour change instantly. Verified in the prototype with reduced motion **and** JavaScript disabled: choosing 24K still re-prices the hero, chip and touchstone card.

### 4.2 The streak and the C-ring

- **Streak.** Under the numeral, four stacked spans, one per karat, each filled with its assay colour and masked by a same-origin `streak.svg` (a rubbed, dry-brush mark with specks at the tail). The checked karat's span wipes in with `clip-path: inset(0 0 0 0)` over 900 ms after a 220 ms delay while the outgoing one retracts in 380 ms, so changing karat looks like a fresh rub on the stone. Mask support is Baseline (Chrome 120, Safari 15.4, Firefox 53). Reduced motion: the streak simply changes colour.
- **Halo.** The brand's C-ring (the logo's open circle, gap at 50°) drawn as a 1 px SVG circle around the hero piece and the piece-page photo in `--assay`. `pathLength="100"`, `stroke-dasharray: 88 112`, `stroke-dashoffset` from 88 to 0 over 2.4 s, once. It sits behind the photo (`z-index: -1` in an isolated box), so it shows through the feathered velvet and never covers the jewellery. Universal SVG support. Reduced motion: drawn complete.
- **Seam sheen.** The 1 px bronze seam between the stage hero and the plum estimator carries one sheen on load: a registered `--sheen` percentage sweeps a champagne highlight along the rule over 2.2 s, once.

### 4.3 The old-gold estimator

- **Mechanics.** A `<form>` with no submit: a range input styled as a scale beam (square thumb rotated 45° in `--assay`, ticks every 10 g, labelled 0 to 200), a number input for exact grams with minus and plus steppers, and four karat chips that are the page karat (choosing 21K here re-prices the hero and the header chip too). The result rolls with the same digit roller, rounded to whole dirhams; under it the exact sum: "55 g × AED 449.00 per gram (21K) = AED 24,695.00". A branch select composes the WhatsApp message ("I have about 55 g of 21K old gold and would like to bring it to the Rolla Mall, Sharjah branch to be weighed").
- **Honesty.** "Indicative value at today's rate", "As of 12:02", and "An estimate, not an offer. The final figure is set at the counter after testing." The method is the promise already in `site.ts` (`gold-exchange`: credited by weight at the day's rate against a new piece), which is still `TODO(owner)`: if the owner applies a deduction or a buying rate, the formula gains one named parameter and the line says so.
- **JavaScript.** About 1.3 KB minified (`estimator.ts`). Without JS the instrument shows the baked example (10 g of 22K, AED 4,683) and the WhatsApp button still works with that message. The sum line is `aria-live="polite"`.

### 4.4 Bar, touchstone, necklace (the assembled heritage)

- **Mechanics.** An `<ol>` of three dark cases on ivory: a drawn bullion bar engraved "999.9 FINE GOLD" (inline SVG in `--assay-24`, a line drawing rather than a fake photograph), a touchstone with the four karat streaks (the selected karat lit), and p03 on its own stage with a radial feather. The list declares `view-timeline: --cases block`; each card animates from `translate(var(--fx), var(--fy)) rotate(var(--fr))` and transparent to its place over `entry 0% cover 42%`. Below 64 em each card uses its own `view()` so the stacked column assembles card by card. Each card ends with a live rate line (24K for the bar, the selected karat for the stone, 22K for the necklace), and a bronze hairline joins the tiles on desktop.
- **Support.** Scroll-driven animations: Chrome 115, Safari 26 (compositor-threaded from 26.4); Firefox and older iPhones get the assembled row. Zero JS.
- **Reduced motion.** Assembled, static.

### 4.5 The line between the counters

- **Mechanics.** A schematic, west-to-east line: Madinat Zayed, two hollow markers for Musaffah / Al Ain and MBZ City ("opening soon"), the Deira flagship as a filled diamond in `--assay`, then Rolla Mall and Safari Mall. The stations sit exactly on the column dividers of the four branch cards below, so each card hangs from its station. The line is a 1.5 px bar animated with `scale: 0 1` to `1 1` on a `view-timeline` (transform only, so it runs on the compositor, unlike a `stroke-dashoffset` draw); stations pop (`scale: 0` to `1`) in four staggered ranges, all complete by the time the route has fully entered. Labelled "Schematic, west to east. Not to scale." On phones the line turns vertical (pseudo-elements on each card), static.
- **Reduced motion / unsupported.** Fully drawn.

### 4.6 The piece grows into its page (supporting moment)

The hero photo and the piece page photo share `view-transition-name: piece-cj-nk-005`; the header and the action bar are named so they hold still. `@view-transition { navigation: auto }` inside `no-preference`. 0 B of JavaScript; Chrome 126 and Safari 18.2, about 88 % of users; Firefox navigates normally. In production the name comes from the product slug, and a `Speculation-Rules` header prerenders on hover so the morph runs between two finished pages.

## 10. Performance and accessibility

**Performance**

- **LCP** is the hero photo (p05, 600w AVIF about 15 KB, the page's only `priority` image) or the numeral text. Neither is ever faded, scaled up or given `@starting-style`; the halo draws around the photo and the recede only runs on exit.
- **CLS 0.** The roller columns are fixed `1em` windows with a hidden width digit and tabular lining figures, so a karat change or a live refresh never moves layout; `StageImage` has `aspect-ratio: 1` independent of its image; the ledger and chip have fixed metrics.
- **Fonts.** Preload only Cormorant roman (the numeral). The hero H1 uses the italic and the interface uses Plex, so three files render above the fold: subset both families to Latin and instance the variable axes to 300 to 500 (Cormorant) and 400 to 600 (Plex) to keep the three under 90 KB, with Astro's metric-matched fallbacks preventing swap shift. If the budget bites, set the H1 in roman.
- **Paint.** Masks only on images of 600 px or less (at most about ten per viewport); scroll-linked work is transform and opacity only (the route uses `scale`, not `stroke-dashoffset`); the two paint effects run once. The registered `--assay` transition restyles the page for 600 ms only on a click.
- **Weight.** Prototype home: 107 KB HTML with all CSS inline (22.6 KB gzip). In production CSS moves to the global file and page HTML falls to about the current 18 KB gzip. Velvet images on home: eight at about 12 to 16 KB AVIF.

**Accessibility**

- One H1 per page; real text for every figure; the roller is `aria-hidden` with per-karat live text.
- Native controls everywhere: radio needles in a `fieldset` with a legend, range and number inputs with labels, `select` for branch, `popover` menus with Esc and focus return.
- Focus rings: plum on light, champagne on dark and plum; 44 px minimum targets (needles are about 100 px tall).
- Contrast: every pairing in §3.2 passes AA; muted text uses colour tokens, never opacity.
- Hover is never the only route: the styles preview also responds to focus and tap, and every name is a link; on phones it is a rail of cards.
- Reduced motion: every animation off, scroll timelines never attached, the page complete. Without JavaScript the karat switch still works and the estimator shows a worked example.
- Prices are not shown for pieces (no fabricated offers in JSON-LD); `Product` schema stays as built.

## 13. How the prototype was checked

Rendered in headless Chrome (CDP) and in a browser-pane tab at 1440 × 900, 1024 × 768, 768 × 1024 and 390 × 844; interactions exercised by script (karat 18K and 24K in the hero, 55 g of 21K in the estimator, the Turkish row in the styles index, the mega menu); repeated with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and JavaScript disabled. No console errors, no horizontal overflow, no image drawn above its natural width, every rate figure traced to `src/content/rates/2026-10-13.json` and every fact to `src/data/site.ts` or `src/content/`.
