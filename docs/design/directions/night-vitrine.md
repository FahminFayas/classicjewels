# Night vitrine

Prototypes (served by the running Astro dev server; it does not serve a folder index, so use the file names):

- Home: http://localhost:4321/_proto/night-vitrine/index.html
- Piece page: http://localhost:4321/_proto/night-vitrine/piece.html (any velvet piece with `?p=p01` to `?p=p09`, default `p03`)

Source: `public/_proto/night-vitrine/` (two self-contained HTML files, `img/` copied from `src/assets/`, `fonts/` copied from `@fontsource-variable`). Checked at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, with and without `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 1. Concept

**Night vitrine.** Walking into the best shop in the Gold Souq after dark: the street is dark, the cases are lit, and the gold is the brightest thing in sight.

**Thesis.** The product photographs were shot at night, in effect: white and teal busts on indigo velvet, 600 px square. Every attempt to put them on white tiles or plum fights them. So the site becomes the room they were shot in. Its ground is the velvet's own darkness (`#0b0918`, sampled from the corners). Each piece is a lit object in a pool of light: a radial mask cut from its own photo, never a rectangle and never larger than 600 CSS px. The live gold rate is the hero instrument and the only thing on the site that moves by itself, and only when the number really changes. Plum is held back for the moments a visitor asks for something: the estimator, the enquiry button, the footer. Asking happens in daylight: when it is time to enquire, the surface turns ivory with a plum button, which is exactly the store's own look. Ivory bands are used sparingly, to let the eye rest on a long dark page. Motion is slow and luminous: case lights come on as you pass, a sheen runs along the rate's hairline when the rate changes, and a line is drawn between Italy and Singapore through Dubai. Nothing loops, and nothing advances on its own.

**Scorecard target (analysis §3).** 8/10 with today's assets. It meets the three things every 8+ site does. There is one idea per screen, from the rate and the cases to the styles line, the windows, the scale and the doors. The motion never moves the layout. A person is the primary action everywhere: WhatsApp, the call button and the branch doors. A ninth point needs the branch photographs and the hero re-shoot (§6.3, items 1 and 2), which slot into the panorama and stage components without design changes.

## 4. Signature moments

### 4.1 The rate instrument (hero)

- **What happens.** Today's 22K rate sits at 200 px in champagne Cormorant 300, with "AED" and "per gram" set small beside it. Beneath a bronze hairline sit four karat keys (24K, 22K, 21K, 18K) with their values always visible. Pressing a key makes it the large figure, and its digits roll to the new value. When the idle refresh in `rate.ts` returns a value that differs from the baked one, only the digits that changed roll. They roll up for a rise and down for a fall, with a 55 ms stagger from the right. A one-time sheen runs along the hairline (in production it also runs once, 600 ms after load), and the status line gains "Down AED 3.00 since 12:02". The header chip, the action bar, the drawer, the footer, the piece ticket and the estimator all update from the same values.
- **Mechanics.** The baked value is plain text in the HTML, so it is correct with no JavaScript. The visible digits are `aria-hidden`, and a `.sr-only` twin carries "AED 465.25 per gram". To roll a digit, it is wrapped in `.d` (inline-block, `overflow: clip`, a soft vertical mask) holding a hidden ghost digit, which fixes the width, and a two-item reel animated with `translate` (compositor only). After 760 ms plus the stagger, the digits are set back to plain text. Tabular lining figures keep the width constant: the prototype measured the same 236.8 px before and during a roll (CLS 0). The sheen is a 34 %-wide gradient strip translated across a 1 px clipped rule, once. Keys are `<button aria-pressed>`; the status line is `role="status"`.
- **Support and fallback.** Everything is CSS animation and `translate` (universal). `text-box` trims the numeral in Chrome 133+ and Safari 18.2+; elsewhere the "AED" label sits slightly high. Without JavaScript the keys are inert list items in production (the component renders spans and `rate.ts` upgrades them), and every value is visible.
- **Reduced motion.** Digits swap in place, no sheen, and the delta text appears without a fade.
- **Honesty rules.** It never counts up on load. It rolls only when the stored value differs from the fetched one, or when the visitor presses a key. The time shown is the time of the fetch, never a typed one.

### 4.2 Walking past the cases

- **What happens.** The eight velvet necklaces hang in a staggered wall (columns 2 and 4 drop by 128 px; on phones the right column drops by 64 px). Each case starts dim: a tight, low pool of light. As it nears the middle of the screen, its light comes on: the pool widens and brightens to the full spot. By the time a visitor's eye reaches a case, it is lit. Hover or focus draws a champagne line along the case's plinth and wipes the underline. Nothing on the photo moves or scales.
- **Mechanics.** Two `<img>` of the same file (one download): `.lit__base` with `--mask-dim` at 0.55 opacity, and `.lit__light` with `--mask-spot`. The light layer has `animation: light-on` (opacity only) on `animation-timeline: view()` with `animation-range: entry 55% cover 48%`. The case text rises 24 px on its own `view()` range. The containers use `overflow: clip`, never `hidden`. The cap is `--cap: 420px`, under the 600 px source.
- **Support and fallback.** Chrome 115+ and Safari 26+ (about 87 %). Firefox and older iPhones show every case lit, which is the finished design.
- **Reduced motion.** All cases lit and static.
- **Measured.** At scroll position 2000 on a 406 px pane, the cases at the top of the viewport read opacity 1.00, the one entering read 0.17, and those below read 0.

### 4.3 From Italy to Singapore, with Dubai between

- **What happens.** The seven goldsmithing traditions are placed on a single bronze line by the longitude of where they come from. Italian sits at 12°E, Turkish (Istanbul) at 29°E, Kuwaiti at 48°E, Rajkot at 71°E, Temple (Tamil Nadu) at 78°E, Kolkata at 88°E and Singapore at 104°E. Dubai sits at 55°E, marked with the C-ring and labelled "Our four branches". As the section enters, the line draws outward from Dubai in both directions, and the names arrive in order of their distance from Dubai. Each name links to its collection, with its origin line, karats and degrees. It answers the question no UAE jeweller answers (analysis §4.3): why a Deira counter carries Kolkata, Rajkot, Temple, Turkish, Kuwaiti, Italian and Singapore work.
- **Mechanics.** Positions are `--x` percentages computed at build time from a `longitude` field on each collection, as (lon − 12) / 92. Labels alternate above and below the line to avoid collisions; the first and last are edge-aligned. The two line segments are `<i>` elements scaled from `scale: 0 1` with transform origins at Dubai, on a named `view-timeline: --lon`. Labels fade on the same timeline, with ranges offset by `--d` (distance rank) using `calc()` in `animation-range`. Under 64em the line turns vertical and the items stack west to east, top to bottom.
- **Support and fallback.** Same as §4.2; the static state is the complete line.
- **Honesty.** Captioned "Schematic: each tradition is placed by the longitude of the place it comes from". Degrees are rounded city longitudes. Antique, Gemstone, Diamonds, Kids and Customised are listed beneath, because they are not places.

### 4.4 Old gold, on today's scale

- **What happens.** On a plum band (an enquiry moment), the visitor sets grams (with − and + steppers) and a karat. The indicative value rolls to the new figure in champagne, for example "AED 4,652.50", with "10 g × AED 465.25, today's 22K rate" underneath. The WhatsApp button, "Bring it to a branch", is prefilled with the grams and karat the visitor entered.
- **Mechanics.** A native `<form>` with no submit: a number input, a `fieldset` of radios styled as a segmented control (`:has(input:checked)`), and an `<output aria-live="polite">` with screen-reader text. The roll is the same function as §4.1 with a 220 ms debounce while typing. It listens for the `cj:rates` event, so a rate refresh re-rolls it.
- **Copy guard.** "An indication, not a quote. The credit is set at the counter after testing, and goes against a new piece. Ask us for the terms before you come." The steps reuse `site.ts` and the exchange FAQ: tested in front of you, weighed with stones and clasps set aside, credited at the day's rate for its karat. There are no percentages, and "no weight deduction" appears only as the `site.ts` promise title, pending owner sign-off.

### 4.5 The piece grows into its page

- **What happens.** Tapping a case or the hero piece cross-fades the page in 240 ms. The header and the action bar hold still, and the piece's photograph travels from its case into the stage of the piece page.
- **Mechanics.** `@view-transition { navigation: auto; }` inside `no-preference`. The header has `view-transition-name: hdr` and the bar `bar`. A 0.25 KB click handler sets `view-transition-name: piece` on the clicked case image only. The piece page's stage image always carries `piece`. ## 10. Performance and accessibility

**LCP under 2 s on mobile.** The LCP is the hero photograph (600w AVIF, about 15 KB) or the numeral text, whichever paints larger. One `priority` image per page. The Cormorant roman is the only preload (37.6 KB). The italic arrives later for the H1 ampersand and the accents, swapping through Astro's metric-matched fallback; if a trace shows the italic on the critical path, the H1 ampersand is set in roman. Nothing in the first viewport has a reveal, `@starting-style` or opacity 0. The hero piece's recede runs only in the exit range.

**CLS 0.** Every image has width and height and a fixed `aspect-ratio`. Rate figures are tabular, and the roll keeps a ghost digit, so the width was measured identical during a roll. The rate line, delta and chip never insert content above other content.

**Paint cost.** Masks only on images of 600 px or less, at most eight masked images per viewport. No animated masks: the light-on is an opacity change of a second masked layer. Blur only on the header's `::before`. Scroll-driven work is opacity and translate only, on the compositor in Chromium and in Safari 26.4+.

**Weight.** The prototype's home CSS is 41.5 KB minified, 8.6 KB gzipped (inside the 20 KB gz budget). The eight velvet JPEGs (29 to 49 KB each) become 11 to 16 KB AVIFs through `<Picture>`. Base and light layers share one URL.

**Accessibility.**

- One H1 per page. The home H1 is factual for search; the H2s name sections in two clauses.
- Real text everywhere: no copy is baked into images.
- Focus rings are champagne on night and plum on day, 2 px with a 3 px offset. Targets are at least 44 px; the bar cells are 56 px.
- Every hover has a focus equivalent: the mega preview uses `:focus-within`, and underline wipes trigger on `:focus-visible`. The case lights depend on scroll position, never on the pointer.
- Popovers give Escape and light-dismiss natively, and invokers mirror `aria-expanded`.
- Rate: visible digits are `aria-hidden` with a screen-reader twin, and the status line is `role="status"`. The estimator uses `fieldset` and `legend` with an `output` live region. "AED" is real text (the U+20C3 dirham sign has no font support yet).
- Reduced motion yields the finished static page.
- Contrast is in §3.2. Body text on night stays at weight 400 and 17 px, at 13.7:1 rather than pure white, to limit halation.
- `forced-colors`: buttons keep a 1 px border, and the masks are ignored by the system palette.
- Print: a print stylesheet flips every tone to day.
