# SEO strategy: competitors, keyword clusters, structured data policy

> Research gathered 13 October 2026. Competitor pages were fetched and parsed; Google policies were read from Search Central. Search volumes are tiered estimates, not tool data.

## Summary

Researched 5 UAE jewellers (Malabar UAE, Damas, Kalyan UAE, Joyalukkas UAE, Meena Jewellers; plus Bafleh as a Deira single-brand baseline) by downloading raw HTML of home, category, product, gold-rate and store pages and parsing <title>/meta/canonical/hreflang/H1/JSON-LD. Only Malabar and Damas ship Product JSON-LD (both with price). Kalyan's JewelryStore JSON-LD is invalid JSON (JS comments) and carries its Indian HQ address on the UAE site; Joyalukkas UAE is a client-rendered SPA with no <title>, meta or JSON-LD in the HTML and loads its gold rate via a 'getgoldrates' API; Damas has no gold-rate page at all; Malabar's SSR gold-rate page renders a stale 2017 Kerala placeholder. None of them publishes a crawlable, per-karat Dubai rate table with a timestamp in static HTML, and none targets Deira/Gold Souq Extension locally. That is the opening for Classic Jewels: a statically built daily rate page (24/22/21/18/14K, AED/g, DJG-aligned update times), locally targeted titles, a clean JewelryStore graph, and piece pages that show karat/weight/hallmark. Google policy facts verified from Search Central: Product markup without price is valid but earns no rich result (needs offers.price, review or aggregateRating); merchant listings need a purchasable page and price > 0; FAQ rich results were removed May 7 2026 (docs deleted June 15 2026); sitelinks search box removed Nov 21 2024; LocalBusiness requires name+address, geo to 5 dp, hours hh:mm:ss, priceRange <100 chars; self-serving aggregateRating on LocalBusiness is not allowed. Keyword clusters (17 EN + Arabic + tourist) are delivered with intent, target URL, title (<=60) and meta (<=155). Search-volume tooling was not available, so volumes are tiered estimates (low confidence); intents and SERP shape are verified. Owner must confirm: opening hours, canonical email, geo coordinates, 'Mariyam' vs 'Maryam' Plaza spelling, Planet Tax Free registration, Bareeq status, exact 0%-deduction terms, and the '30+ years' claim (group site says 35 years in gold, 16 years in UAE, and a stats block says 12).

## Recommendations

- URL architecture (lowercase, hyphenated, trailing slash, no .html): / , /gold-rate/ , /jewellery/ (all pieces) , /jewellery/{necklaces|earrings|rings|pendants|bangles|bracelets|chains|anklets}/ , /jewellery/{category}/{slug}-{sku}/ (piece pages; canonical = this URL, unlike Malabar's category-less canonical) , /gold-jewellery/ (karat hub: 22K/18K explained + links) , /diamond-jewellery/ , /uncut-diamond-jewellery/ , /gemstone-jewellery/ , /gold-coins-and-bars/ , /collections/{temple|kolkata|rajkot|antique|kuwait|singapore|turkish|italian|kids|kerala}/ , /occasions/{wedding|anniversary|birthday|festivals}/ , /gifts/ , /custom-jewellery/ , /gold-exchange/ , /visit/ (store, hours, directions, parking, VAT refund) , /promise/{916-hallmark|certified-diamonds|returns-and-maintenance|gold-exchange-terms}/ , /about/ , /contact/ , /journal/ , /journal/{slug}/ , /news/ , /gallery/ , /privacy/ , /terms/ , /sitemap-index.xml , /robots.txt. Reserve /ar/ mirrored 1:1 for later; hreflang en-AE + x-default now, add ar-AE only when native copy ships.
- Make the gold rate crawlable: fetch https://classicjewels.ae/gold-rates.php at build time and render the 24K/22K/21K/18K/14K table into static HTML with <time datetime> and 'AED per gram' in the markup; schedule rebuilds daily (and ideally at the DJG update slots 09:00/13:30/18:00 GST) via the host's deploy hook; the vanilla-TS island then refreshes client-side and only replaces numbers if gold_rate_date is newer. Also persist each day's feed into a 'rates' content collection so /gold-rate/ can grow a 7/30-day table and later month pages — history is the one thing news sites and Malabar do not offer per shop. Check the terms of the 'Subscription Valid' rate provider before publishing.
- JSON-LD graph (one <script> per page, @graph with stable @ids): JewelryStore @id https://classicjewels.ae/#store {name 'Classic Jewels & Diamonds', legalName (confirm), url, logo ImageObject >=112px on white, image[] (shopfront + interior), telephone '+97142551288', contactPoint[{contactType 'sales', telephone '+971505661416', contactOption 'TollFree' omitted, availableLanguage ['en','ar','hi','ml'] (confirm)}], email (confirm canonical), address{streetAddress 'Shop No. 6, Mariyam Plaza (Multistorey Car Parking Building), Gold Souq Extension, behind RAK Bank', addressLocality 'Deira', addressRegion 'Dubai', addressCountry 'AE'}, geo{latitude,longitude 5+ dp from GBP}, hasMap 'https://www.google.com/maps?cid=14419653802905195883', openingHoursSpecification[] (owner data; hh:mm:ss), priceRange 'AED 300 – AED 50,000+' (<100 chars; or 'AED 300–50000'), currenciesAccepted 'AED', paymentAccepted 'Cash, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Maestro, PayPal', areaServed ['Dubai','United Arab Emirates'], foundingDate '2024', parentOrganization{@type Organization, name 'Classic Group of Companies', url 'https://classicgroup.ae/'}, sameAs [Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Threads, Google Maps URL], slogan 'Every Jewel Has A Tale', hasMerchantReturnPolicy{applicableCountry 'AE', returnPolicyCategory 'MerchantReturnFiniteReturnWindow', merchantReturnDays 14, returnMethod 'ReturnInStore', returnFees 'FreeReturn'}}; WebSite {name, alternateName 'Classic Jewels', url} with NO SearchAction; BreadcrumbList on every non-home page; Product on piece pages; BlogPosting on journal; NewsArticle on news; ImageGallery on gallery; no FAQPage (feature removed May 2026; keep FAQs as semantic <details>/<h3> HTML). Never add aggregateRating/review to the JewelryStore.
- Product JSON-LD decision (owner must choose): Option A (default, no prices on site): Product {name, image[], description, sku, brand{Brand 'Classic Jewels & Diamonds'}, category 'Jewellery > Necklaces', material 'Yellow gold 22K (916)', color, weight{QuantitativeValue value unitCode 'GRM'}, additionalProperty [{name 'Karat', value '22'}, {name 'Fineness', value '916'}, {name 'Net gold weight', value '8.13 g'}], itemCondition NewCondition, isRelatedTo collection URL} with NO offers — valid, supports entity/AI understanding, but Search Console will flag 'offers/review/aggregateRating' and no rich result appears; document this in README. Option B (recommended if owner agrees to show indicative prices): compute price = today's rate x net weight + making charge (+5% VAT) at the daily build, show it visibly on the page as 'Today's indicative price', and emit offers{price, priceCurrency 'AED', availability 'https://schema.org/InStoreOnly', priceValidUntil = today ISO date, seller @id #store, availableAtOrFrom @id #store, url} — this is eligible for product snippets (not merchant listings) and is honest because the number is in the HTML. Do not emit an Offer without price, and never fake price 0.
- On-page SEO contract per page type: unique <title> <=60 chars with the local modifier ('Dubai' or 'Deira') and brand suffix ' | Classic Jewels Deira' or ' | Classic Jewels'; unique meta description <=155 chars with one concrete fact (karat/hallmark/rate/location) and one CTA (WhatsApp/visit); exactly one H1 matching the title's head term; canonical self; og:title/og:description/og:image (1200x630 brand image per section, product image on piece pages); robots index,follow (delete both legacy robots metas); <html lang='en-AE'>; breadcrumb nav in HTML matching the BreadcrumbList; British spelling 'jewellery' everywhere including slugs.

Is it available today?' URL-encoded into https://wa.me/971505661416?text=..., priceMode ('rate' | 'quote'), makingChargeAEDPerGram optional (for Option B), visible 'Priced at today's {karat}K rate' line linking to /gold-rate/.
- Gold-rate page content outline (H2s): Today's rate table (24K, 22K, 21K, 18K, 14K; AED/gram; last updated time) -> How our rate is set (DJG recommended retail rate, updated during the day; we show the latest at build + live refresh) -> What you pay for jewellery (rate x net weight + making charge + 5% VAT, with a worked 8 g 22K example) -> Coins and bars (near-rate pricing, no making charge; link) -> Exchanging old gold (link to /gold-exchange/) -> Rate history (grows from the 'rates' collection) -> Questions (plain HTML: how often it changes, why shop price differs from spot, which karat to buy, is gold cheaper in Dubai than India, can tourists reclaim VAT).
- Local SEO hygiene: fix the GBP name to 'Classic Jewels & Diamonds' (remove the embedded address), primary category 'Jewelry store', secondaries 'Gold dealer', 'Diamond dealer', 'Jewelry buyer' (only if cash-buying old gold is offered), 'Jewelry designer' (custom); set GBP website to https://classicjewels.ae/visit/ (or home) and add UTM-free links; keep NAP byte-identical across site footer, /visit/, JSON-LD, GBP, Instagram bio, Facebook; resolve 'Mariyam' vs 'Maryam' Plaza with the owner (use the GBP spelling in structured data, mention the other once in body copy); resolve the two emails (hello@classicgroup.ae vs c1@classicgrp.ae) to one monitored address; publish the exact '0% deduction' terms (exchange vs cash, karats, excluded items) before using the claim in titles.
- Technical SEO baseline to beat the chains on: robots.txt with Sitemap: line; sitemap-index.xml split by type (pages, products, journal) via @astrojs/sitemap with lastmod from content; 301 map from every old URL (index.php?page=..., /ar) to new paths; security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy) at the host; self-hosted fonts with font-display: swap and size-adjust to hit CLS 0; width/height on every image; preload LCP hero; no third-party scripts before interaction (defer analytics via a consented, lightweight loader); Lighthouse CI in the build.
- Content calendar for /journal/ (Article schema, author = named person at Classic, dateModified maintained): 'Buying gold in Dubai: a Deira jeweller's guide' (tourist cluster), 'How to read a UAE gold hallmark (999/916/875/750)', 'Making charges explained with real examples', 'VAT refund on gold for tourists: step by step' (Planet, 85% minus AED 4.80/tag, AED 250 minimum, 90 days), '22K vs 21K vs 18K: which to buy for wear, gifts and investment', 'Temple jewellery: what the motifs mean', 'Kolkata vs Rajkot work: how to tell them apart', 'Dhanteras/Akshaya Tritiya/Eid: when to buy and what moves the rate', 'Exchanging old gold: what we test and how we weigh'. Each links to the relevant category, collection and /gold-rate/.
- Owner confirmations required before build (block on these): opening hours incl. Friday split; canonical email; lat/long from GBP; legal name + licence number + TRN for legalName/vatID; Planet Tax Free registration (yes/no) for the tourist page; Bareeq certification (yes/no); exact exchange/return/maintenance terms; the defensible 'years' figure (35 years in gold / 16 years in UAE per group site vs '30+' in brief); whether 'Kerala' is a live collection; which diamond lab certificates are issued; whether the rate feed subscription permits public display; whether indicative pricing (Option B) is acceptable.
- Measurement: register Search Console (domain property), submit sitemap index, add the gold-rate page and store page as GBP 'products'/posts targets; track the Product snippets report only if Option B is chosen; set up weekly rank checks for the 20 head terms in clusters 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6; annotate rate-page traffic against festival dates.

### How the Dubai retail gold rate is actually set (for the rate page's explanatory copy and update cadence)

_Confidence: medium_

The Dubai Gold & Jewellery Group / Dubai Jewellery Group (DJG) issues the recommended retail rate per gram for 24K, 22K, 21K and 18K in AED, derived from DGCX/LBMA market data plus a set retail margin, displayed on electronic boards at member stores and supervised by Dubai's Commercial Compliance & Consumer Protection Department. Reported update cadence conflicts between sources: 'three times a day, at 9:00 AM, 1:30 PM, and 6:00 PM UAE time' vs 'four times a day, Monday-Friday'. Khaleej Times 21 Aug 2026 cited DJG: 24K Dh547, 22K Dh506.5, 21K Dh485.75, 18K Dh416.25. Classic's own feed on 2026-10-13 returned 24K 505.75, 22K 468.25, 21K 449.00, 18K 384.75, 14K 300.25 (ratios 22/24 = 0.926, 21/24 = 0.888, 18/24 = 0.761 match DJG's typical spread, so the feed is a DJG-style retail rate in AED per gram, not spot). The feed carries no unit, no time of day, and no 'buying' rate.

Sources: https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/dubai-gold-rate-set-to-be-endorsed-by-government-agency-1.1629392 ; https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/dubai-gold-prices-rise-further-jump-nearly-dh60-per-gram-this-month-so-far ; https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/gold-rate-dubai ; https://classicjewels.ae/gold-rates.php (fetched 2026-10-13)
