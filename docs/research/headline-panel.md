# Home hero headline: the panel

16 October 2026. Four writers (proof, heritage, brand voice, straight value) each proposed five or six display lines for the home H1 after reading the competitor notes in `docs/research/competitive/`; three judges (a Sharjah mother buying wedding gold and an Abu Dhabi gift buyer, a maison creative director, an SEO and conversion lead) scored every line out of 10. The old site's three lines and both redesign lines sat in the same pool.

## What the competitors lead with

Malabar has no headline in live text at all: seven celebrity posters with the copy baked into the JPG, then a carousel of promises ("100% value on gold exchange") and "450+ showrooms across 14 countries". Joyalukkas puts "World's favourite jeweller" under its logo and opens on a festive price poster (pay 10% advance, lowest-rate guarantee); its only H1 is an SEO essay at the foot of the page. Kalyan's UAE hero was broken; its gold hub says "pieces that tell a story". Bhima leads with "my kind of jewellery" and its 1925 founding year, Pure Gold with "Premium Collection", Jos Alukkas and Mahallati with a founding year over a film. The maisons (Cartier, Van Cleef, Bvlgari) lead with one object and a short sentence, or no words. Nobody in the set opens on something a customer can check at the counter, and three of them reach for the same "story" metaphor the old Classic slogan uses.

## Verdict on the old lines and the current line

- "Where Elegance Meets Eternity": two banned words, title case, and its sub-line leans on De Beers's "A diamond is forever". It could hang over any jeweller. Score 5/30.
- "A Treasure As Unique As You": a greeting card; nothing about gold, Dubai, weight or rate. Score 6/30.
- "Every Jewel Has A Tale": the registered mark and the only old line worth keeping, but soft, unplaced, and already used by Kalyan and Joyalukkas in passing. Alone it scores 12/30. It stays as the site's sign-off voice (footer, about page).
- "Hallmarked gold, weighed in front of you": better than all three old lines on every judge's sheet (19/30), because it is checkable and in the voice. Its weaknesses: "hallmarked" is table stakes and already on the same screen twice (the 916 key, the fact strip); "weighed in front of you" is what every honest Deira counter does and already lives on the piece ticket; it describes a transaction, not a reason to want the necklace lit beside it.

## Recommendation

Ship **"Every jewel has a tale, _and a hallmark_"** (40 characters, two lines on a 390 px phone). It is the one line the chains cannot copy, because the slogan is Classic's, and it turns the slogan from a greeting card into a claim the screen then proves: the tale is the necklace in its light, the hallmark is the 916 key in the instrument. The kicker stays "Gold and diamond jewellers in Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi" for search.

If the owner prefers proof over warmth, the alternative is **"Every gram weighed, _every stone tested_"**. "Hallmarked gold, weighed in front of you" moves to where it is strongest: the piece ticket and the promise band.

The line is one span in `src/components/home/HomeHero.astro` (the `.hero__line` element) and one row in `docs/design-system-v2.md` §8.2.
