Executive summary
Classic Jewels & Diamonds is a young retail brand with an older trading business behind it. It launched in 2024 as
the jewellery division of Classic Group, whose first business, Classic Bullion, trades bullion [4] [Verified]. In under
two years it has grown from one showroom in Deira’s Gold Souq Extension to four branches across Dubai,
Sharjah and Abu Dhabi, with more announced [11][12][33] [Verified]. Customers rate the Deira store 4.9 out of 5
across 680 Google reviews [6] [Verified].
The website tells almost none of this story, and Google cannot see it. On 13 October every page carried a
noindex tag, a site: search on Google returned no pages, and not one of the homepage’s category, collection or
product tiles led anywhere [1][36] [Verified]. The audience is on Instagram, with 25K followers, and in the stores
[10]. The redesign’s job is to turn that reach, and the gold-trade credibility, into pages people can find, browse and
enquire from.
The positioning conclusion from the first edition stands. Most of what the brand advertises as assurances is what
every major UAE jeweller also offers. Its real edge, if the team confirms it, sits in the gold trade itself: value on
exchange, competitive making charges and a trader’s credibility on gold. The redesigned site should lead with
that and link every claim to proof.
Five things to know
Fix indexing before anything else. Every page has a noindex tag, and the homepage carries two conflicting
robots tags; Google applies the stricter one [1][38]. A site: search returned nothing and a brand search does not
show the site [36][37]. The fix takes minutes and needs no redesign.
Visitors cannot see a single product. The homepage has 199 links to “#”, including every category, collection
and product tile. There are no product pages, prices or per-piece enquiries [1]. An enquiry-led catalogue is the
core of the redesign.
The best feature is hidden. The live gold rate works, but it sits in a dropdown on desktop and inside the menu
on mobile [1]. Both big chains give the rate its own page [41][43]; Classic should too, with a visible bar on every
page.
The trust badges are table stakes, and none has written terms. Lifetime maintenance, exchange value,
certified diamonds and 916 hallmarking match the chains’ promises, and all six badges lead nowhere [1][24][27].
Policy pages are launch-critical.
Contact details disagree. The homepage shows hello@classicgroup.ae and the other five pages
c1@classicgrp.ae. Google’s listing uses 050 551 5826 for calls and WhatsApp, while the website uses 050 566
1416 [1][6]. Agree one set before the redesign, or leads will keep splitting.
